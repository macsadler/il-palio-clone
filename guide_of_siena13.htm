Yup; the coat of arms of the Tolomei is visible above the entrance door of the Palace. It is a triangle with the vertex facing down, which houses three crescent moons within it. Each moon is symbolically associated with a flag stolen from the Turks, or the financing of a crusade. The coat of arms tells us a lot about the power and wealth of a family like this.
